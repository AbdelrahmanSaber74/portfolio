01 — Kuwait · Live

ORO

Gold & Jewelry E-Commerce + Retail POS
Au
price-lock · 60 min window

A gold & jewelry platform where the price of the product literally moves every second. I built the backend on .NET 8 and a 5-layer DDD Clean Architecture — the kind of foundation that survives feature #200.

The problemGram prices swing with the live gold market. A customer who starts checkout must pay the price they saw, not the price 30 minutes later.
The engineA real-time pricing snapshot & locking engine that fixes gram prices for a one-hour window at checkout — absorbing market volatility while guaranteeing the customer a stable, honest total.
Trust & moneyHybrid auth (JWT for members, scoped opaque tokens for guest checkout), MyFatoorah payments (K-Net, cards, links) with webhook reconciliation, customer wallet, vendor balances, and Kuwait KYC/AML — kept consistent by EF Core idempotency locks.
In store tooOmnichannel POS with multi-branch inventory — barcode scanning, piece tracking, breakage sales, and automated PDF invoices & receipts.

02 — Ride-Hailing

Mashaweer

Real-Time Ride-Hailing Platform
⟲
self-hosted · SignalR/WS

A ride-hailing backend that has to be right and instant at the same time — ride requests, live driver tracking, in-app chat, scheduling, payments, and notifications, all moving in real time.

The migrationLed the move of all real-time messaging from paid MQTT/HiveMQ to a self-hosted SignalR-over-WebSockets architecture — removing a recurring third-party cost while taking full control of the pipeline.
Never lose an eventGuaranteed delivery with a durable SQL Outbox, idempotent handling keyed on eventId / version, auto-reconnection, and offline sync — with Firebase push as the fallback channel.
Shipped safelyDeployed through GitLab CI/CD, with Dapper for the hot read paths and JWT securing every socket and endpoint.
